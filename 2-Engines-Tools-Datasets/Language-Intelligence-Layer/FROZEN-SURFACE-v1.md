## Trust Boundary
The Language Intelligence Layer produces advisory output only.
It cannot modify domain truth or make routing decisions.
Normalization and disambiguation outputs are suggestions for practitioner review.
The Commercial Orchestrator may use routing hints but is not bound by them.
